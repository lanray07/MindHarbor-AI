# MindHarbor AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

MindHarbor AI is designed as a private, on-device journal and mental wellbeing companion. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information you create

MindHarbor AI may store information you choose to provide, including journal entries, mood check-ins, tags, voice-journal transcripts, Copilot conversation history, support contacts, and app preferences. This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's platform storage technologies.

MindHarbor AI does not require an account. The current version does not transmit this information to the developer, sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to track you across apps or websites.

## Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose voice journaling, the app requests microphone and speech-recognition permission. These permissions are used only to capture and transcribe the journal entry you initiate. You can decline or revoke either permission in iOS Settings.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission and schedules reminders on your device. Notification preferences can be changed in the app or in iOS Settings.

## Photos and biometrics

The app may request photo-library access only if you choose a feature that attaches media. If you enable app lock, authentication is handled by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. The app does not receive or store your Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode data.

## Data sharing and tracking

The current version of MindHarbor AI does not collect data from the app, share personal data with third parties, include third-party advertising, or perform cross-app tracking.

## Data retention and deletion

Your entries and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the app, remove related content, or uninstall the app. Because the developer does not receive this on-device information, the developer cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

## Children

MindHarbor AI is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Wellbeing notice

MindHarbor AI supports personal reflection. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, crisis response, or emergency services.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.
